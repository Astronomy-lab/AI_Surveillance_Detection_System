-->Phone (for option 2)

*) Install IP Webcam (Android), open it and tap "Start server". The URL looks like http://192.168.1.60:8080/video.
*) Phone and PC must be on the same WiFi.
*) On iPhone, use an app that gives an MJPEG or RTSP URL and paste that URL.

-->Limitations:
*) YOLO can only recognise the 80 COCO classes. A photo of something outside them, like a specific key or a custom toy, gets rejected with a message. For those you would need to train a custom YOLO model on your own photos.
*) Colour matching is simple. Two objects of the same class and similar colour can both match. Raise HIST_THRESH to be stricter.
*) A clear target photo with the object large, centred, and in similar lighting works best.
